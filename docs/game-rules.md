# 6 nimmt! — Rules Summary

Source: <https://www.ultraboardgames.com/6-nimmt/game-rules.php>

## Components

- 104 cards
- Paper and pencil for scorekeeping (not included)

## Objective

Avoid picking up cards. Each bullhead on a card you pick up is one minus point.
The player with the fewest bullheads at the end of the game wins.

## Setup

1. Shuffle all cards and deal 10 cards to each player.
2. Place the next 4 cards face-up on the table. Each one starts a row.
3. Set the remaining cards aside. They are not used until the next round.

A row holds a maximum of 5 cards, including its first card.

## Turn sequence

1. Each player selects one card from their hand and places it face-down.
2. When all players have selected, all cards are revealed.
3. The cards are added to the rows one at a time, from the lowest number to the highest.
4. Repeat until all 10 cards in each hand have been played.

## Placement rules

Each card can go to one row only. These rules define that row.

| # | Rule | Effect |
|---|------|--------|
| 1 | Ascending order | The card must be higher than the current last card of the row. |
| 2 | Smallest difference | Of the rows that rule 1 allows, the card goes to the row whose last card is closest to it. |
| 3 | Full row | If rule 2 puts the card in a row that already has 5 cards, the player takes all 5 cards. The played card becomes the first card of the new row. |
| 4 | Lowest card | If the card is lower than the last card of every row, the player chooses one row and takes all of its cards. The played card becomes the first card of the new row. |

### Example

Rows end in 12, 37, 43 and 58. The played cards are 14, 15, 44 and 61.

- 14 goes after 12 (rule 1 allows only that row).
- 15 goes after 14 in the same row.
- 44 can go in three rows by rule 1. Rule 2 puts it after 43.
- 61 goes after 58.

## Bullheads (minus points)

Each card shows 1 to 7 bullheads.

| Card | Bullheads |
|------|-----------|
| Multiple of 5 that is not a multiple of 10 (5, 15, 25, ...) | 2 |
| Multiple of ten (10, 20, 30, ...) | 3 |
| Doublet (11, 22, 33, ...) | 5 |
| 55 (doublet that is a multiple of 5) | 7 |
| All other cards | 1 |

## Bullhead stack

Cards that a player picks up go face-down in front of that player, in the bullhead stack.
These cards cannot be taken into the hand or played.

## End of a round

A round ends when all players have played all 10 cards.

1. Each player counts the bullheads in their bullhead stack.
2. Record the minus points of each player.
3. Shuffle all cards and start a new round.

## End of the game

The game ends when a player has 66 or more bullheads in total.
The player with the fewest bullheads at that time wins.

Players can agree on a different score limit or on a fixed number of rounds.
